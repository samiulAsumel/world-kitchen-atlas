Thosai (தோசை (Thōcai) — Sinhala rendering: තෝසේ (Those))
Sri Lanka - Breads

A thin, crisp, golden fermented rice-and-lentil crepe, cooked on a hot griddle until lacy and lightly crisp at the edges.

Key ingredients: raw rice, urad dal (split black gram), fenugreek seeds, salt, water, vegetable oil

What it should actually look like: A large, thin, golden-brown crisp crepe, folded or rolled, with a lacy, slightly blistered surface -- thin and crisp, not thick like a pancake.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan thosai", "tamil dosai crepe", "sri lanka dosa breakfast"
