Watalappan (වට්ටලප්පම්)
Sri Lanka - Desserts

A dense, steamed custard of coconut milk, eggs, and kithul jaggery, warmed with cardamom, nutmeg, and cloves and topped with cashews — Sri Lanka's most celebrated festive dessert.

Key ingredients: coconut milk, eggs, kithul jaggery, cashews

What it should actually look like: A wedge or small bowl of a dense, dark caramel-brown custard with a smooth, glossy cut surface and a few cashew halves on top; the deep amber-brown color (not pale like a Western flan) is the key visual cue.

Suggested search terms: "watalappan sri lankan dessert", "coconut jaggery custard", "sri lanka eid pudding"
