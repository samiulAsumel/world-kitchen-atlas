Wattakka Curry (වට්ටක්කා කරිය (Wattakka Kariya))
Sri Lanka - Sides

A mild, golden coconut-milk curry of soft-cooked pumpkin, tempered with mustard seeds, fenugreek, and curry leaves.

Key ingredients: pumpkin, coconut milk, onion, garlic cloves, curry leaves, mustard seeds, fenugreek seeds, turmeric powder

What it should actually look like: A golden-yellow, mildly thick coconut-milk curry with soft, slightly broken-down pumpkin cubes in a shallow bowl -- mild and pale, not deep red or fiery-looking.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan pumpkin curry", "wattakka curry", "coconut milk pumpkin curry"
