Lamprais (ලම්ප්‍රයිස්)
Sri Lanka - Mains

A Dutch Burgher classic: rice cooked in meat stock, mixed meat curry, spiced meatballs, a sweet-sour eggplant relish, and a chili paste, all parceled into a banana leaf and baked until the flavors meld into one dish.

Key ingredients: stock rice, mixed meat curry, meatballs, eggplant relish, banana leaf

What it should actually look like: Either an unwrapped banana-leaf parcel showing rice and several curry components side by side, or the leaf parcel itself slightly opened with steam/aroma implied; the banana leaf itself should be visible and central to the shot.
