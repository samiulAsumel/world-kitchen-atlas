Devilled Chicken (Commonly written phonetically as ඩෙවල්ඩ් චිකන් in Sinhala — this is a direct English loanword rendering rather than an older indigenous term, since the dish itself is a colonial-era fusion creation.)
Sri Lanka - Mains

Crisp-fried chicken pieces tossed in a glossy, spicy-sweet sauce of onions, capsicum, garlic, ginger, and chili, finished with a tangle of spring onion.

Key ingredients: boneless chicken thigh, cornflour, salt and pepper, vegetable oil, onion, capsicum (bell pepper), green chilies, garlic and ginger

What it should actually look like: Glossy, deep red-orange fried chicken pieces tossed with bell pepper and onion petals in a thick, shiny sauce, garnished with spring onion -- served dry-glazed, not swimming in liquid.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan devilled chicken", "devil chicken sri lanka", "spicy fried chicken sri lanka"
