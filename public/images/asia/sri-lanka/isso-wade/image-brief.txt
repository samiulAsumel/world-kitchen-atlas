Isso Wade (Shrimp Fritter) (ඉස්සො වඩේ / இறால் வடை)
Sri Lanka - Snacks (Street Food)

Crisp, savory urad dal fritters with a small whole shrimp pressed into the center before frying -- a classic coastal Sri Lankan street snack, sold hot from roadside and beachfront carts.

Key ingredients: urad dal, onion, green chilies, curry leaves, small whole shrimp, oil for deep frying

What it should actually look like: Deep golden-brown, roughly round fritters with a rough, craggy fried surface, each with a single small pink-orange shrimp (tail often visible) pressed into the top -- piled on a plate or in a paper cone, clearly deep-fried rather than baked, with lime wedges nearby.

Suggested search terms: "isso vade sri lanka", "shrimp vada fritter", "prawn lentil fritter street food"
