Gotu Kola Sambol (ගොටුකොළ සම්බෝලය (Gotukola Sambolaya))
Sri Lanka - Sides

A raw, finely shredded salad of gotu kola leaves tossed with grated coconut, onion, lime juice, and chili — fresh, herbal, and slightly peppery.

Key ingredients: gotu kola leaves, fresh grated coconut, red onion, green chili, Maldive fish flakes, lime juice, salt

What it should actually look like: A small, loose mound of finely shredded dark-green leaves flecked with white grated coconut and red onion, served raw and fresh, not cooked or wilted-looking.

Suggested search terms: "gotu kola sambol", "pennywort salad sri lanka", "sri lankan herb salad"
