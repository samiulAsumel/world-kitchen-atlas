King Coconut Water (Thambili) (තැඹිලි)
Sri Lanka - Drinks

The naturally sweet, electrolyte-rich water from inside a king coconut (thambili), served straight from the shell with a straw -- one of Sri Lanka's most common roadside refreshments.

Key ingredients: king coconut (thambili)

What it should actually look like: A whole, bright orange-husked king coconut with the top freshly chopped off, a straw inserted, ideally shown roadside-stall style, possibly alongside a stack of other uncut king coconuts -- the distinctive orange husk color is the key visual cue that separates it from an ordinary green coconut.

Suggested search terms: "king coconut sri lanka", "thambili orange coconut", "fresh coconut water street vendor"
