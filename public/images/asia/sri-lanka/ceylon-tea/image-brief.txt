Ceylon Tea (සිලෝන් තේ / இலங்கை தேயிலை)
Sri Lanka - Drinks

Sri Lanka's globally renowned black tea, grown across high-, mid-, and low-elevation estates that each produce a distinctly different cup -- brewed strong and enjoyed plain or with milk and sugar to taste.

Key ingredients: Ceylon black tea leaves, water

What it should actually look like: A clear glass teacup or white porcelain cup filled with rich amber-to-reddish brewed black tea, steam visibly rising, with loose tea leaves or a teapot nearby -- no milk clouding the color, showing the tea's natural deep color rather than a milky beige.

Suggested search terms: "ceylon black tea cup", "sri lanka tea brewed", "high grown ceylon tea"
