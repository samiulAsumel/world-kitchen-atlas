Pittu (පිට්ටු (Pittu))
Sri Lanka - Breads

Cylinders of steamed rice flour layered with fresh grated coconut, cooked in a traditional bamboo or metal mould until tender and lightly crumbly.

Key ingredients: roasted rice flour, fresh grated coconut, salt, water

What it should actually look like: A pale white cylindrical log, cut into thick rounds, with visible fine crumbly texture and specks of coconut throughout -- distinctly a steamed, not fried, item.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan pittu", "pittu steamed rice coconut", "pittu breakfast sri lanka"
