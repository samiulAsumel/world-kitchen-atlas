Kavum (කැවුම් (Kavum))
Sri Lanka - Desserts

A deep-fried rice flour and treacle cake with a crisp, dark-brown shell and a soft, sweet interior, traditionally made in a small hemispherical mould.

Key ingredients: rice flour, kithul treacle (or substitute jaggery syrup), ripe banana, salt, coconut oil (or a neutral vegetable oil)

What it should actually look like: Small, dark golden-brown, dome-shaped fried cakes with a glossy, slightly wrinkled shell, piled on a plate or tray -- distinctly darker and more rounded than a doughnut.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan kavum", "kavum oil cake", "sri lanka new year sweets"
