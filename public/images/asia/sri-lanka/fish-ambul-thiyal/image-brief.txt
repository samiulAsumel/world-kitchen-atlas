Fish Ambul Thiyal (අඹුල් තියල්)
Sri Lanka - Mains

A dry, intensely sour and peppery Sri Lankan fish curry, cooked down with goraka and black pepper until the liquid nearly disappears and each piece of fish is coated in a dark, tangy glaze.

Key ingredients: tuna, goraka, black pepper, curry leaves

What it should actually look like: Dark brownish-black, dry-glazed chunks of fish with almost no visible sauce pooling around them, distinctly drier and darker than a typical coconut-milk fish curry.

Suggested search terms: "fish ambul thiyal sri lanka", "sour fish curry sri lankan", "dry tuna curry goraka"
