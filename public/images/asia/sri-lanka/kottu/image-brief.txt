Kottu (කොත්තු (Kottu))
Sri Lanka - Mains

Shredded roti stir-fried on a hot griddle with egg, chicken, vegetables, and spices, chopped into small pieces with two flat blades in a distinctive rhythmic clatter.

Key ingredients: godhamba roti (or leftover flatbread), boneless chicken thigh, eggs, onion, leeks, carrot, cabbage, green chilies

What it should actually look like: Finely chopped, glistening strands of roti mixed with visible flecks of egg, chicken, and colorful vegetables, piled on a plate, steam rising -- not a smooth curry, a chopped stir-fry texture.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan kottu roti", "kottu chicken stir fry", "sri lanka street food kottu"
