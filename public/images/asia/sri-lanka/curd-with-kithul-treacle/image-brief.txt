Curd with Kithul Treacle (මී කිරි සහ කිතුල් පැණි (Mee Kiri saha Kithul Pani))
Sri Lanka - Desserts

Thick, tangy set buffalo milk curd drizzled generously with kithul palm treacle — a simple, iconic Sri Lankan dessert of just two ingredients.

Key ingredients: buffalo milk (or full-fat cow milk), plain yogurt (as a starter culture), kithul treacle

What it should actually look like: A clay pot or bowl of thick, pale ivory-white set curd with a dark amber-brown syrup pooled and dripping over the top -- the contrast between white curd and dark syrup is the key visual.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan curd and treacle", "buffalo curd kithul treacle", "curd and honey sri lanka dessert"
