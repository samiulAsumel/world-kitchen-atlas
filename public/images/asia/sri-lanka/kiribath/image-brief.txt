Kiribath (Milk Rice) (කිරිබත්)
Sri Lanka - Mains

Rice simmered in thick coconut milk until creamy, set into a flat tray and cut into diamonds — Sri Lanka's most symbolically important dish, traditionally the first food eaten to mark a new beginning.

Key ingredients: rice, coconut milk

What it should actually look like: Several white, diamond-shaped pieces of firm milk rice arranged on a plate, often alongside a small mound of reddish lunu miris relish for color contrast; the rice should look firm-cut, not like a loose porridge.

Suggested search terms: "kiribath milk rice sri lanka", "sri lankan new year rice", "coconut milk rice diamonds"
