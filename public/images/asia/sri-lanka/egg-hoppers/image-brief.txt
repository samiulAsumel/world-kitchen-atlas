Egg Hoppers (බිත්තර ආප්ප)
Sri Lanka - Breads

A lacy, bowl-shaped fermented rice-and-coconut-milk pancake with an egg cracked into its soft center and steamed under a lid until the white sets around a yolk left deliberately soft.

Key ingredients: rice batter, coconut milk, egg

What it should actually look like: A bowl-shaped hopper with a whole fried egg visible in the center, white just set and yolk still glossy; crisp golden-brown lacy rim distinguishes it from a flat omelet.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan egg hopper", "bittara appa", "egg hopper soft yolk"
