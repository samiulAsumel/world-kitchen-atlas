Aluwa (අලුවා (Aluwa))
Sri Lanka - Desserts

A dense, fudge-like diamond-cut sweet made from roasted rice flour, treacle, cashew nuts, and cardamom, with a firm, slightly chewy texture.

Key ingredients: rice flour, kithul treacle (or substitute jaggery, ghee, cashew nuts, cardamom powder, salt

What it should actually look like: Small, diamond-cut, dense fudge-like brown pieces studded with visible cashew bits, arranged neatly on a plate -- a solid confection, not a liquid or paste.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan aluwa sweet", "aluwa rice flour sweet", "sri lanka new year sweets"
