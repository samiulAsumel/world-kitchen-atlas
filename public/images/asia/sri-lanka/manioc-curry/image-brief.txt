Manioc Curry (මඤ්ඤොක්කා කරිය (Mañyokka Kariya))
Sri Lanka - Sides

Tender boiled cassava simmered in a mild coconut-milk gravy tempered with mustard seeds, turmeric, and curry leaves.

Key ingredients: cassava (manioc), coconut milk, onion, garlic, curry leaves, mustard seeds, turmeric powder, green chili

What it should actually look like: Pale golden-white cassava chunks in a light, thin coconut-milk gravy in a bowl -- mild and pale, similar to a potato curry but with visibly starchier, denser root vegetable pieces.
