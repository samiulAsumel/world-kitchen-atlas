Kottu Roti (Vegetable & Egg) (කොත්තු රොටී / Koththu Roti)
Sri Lanka - Snacks (Street Food)

The plain, everyday version of kottu -- shredded roti chopped and stir-fried on a hot griddle with egg, onion, leeks, and cabbage, no meat required, sold from roadside stalls across Sri Lanka after dark.

Key ingredients: godhamba roti, eggs, onion, leeks, carrot, cabbage, green chilies, curry leaves, curry powder, soy sauce

What it should actually look like: A steaming pile of finely chopped, ribbon-like roti pieces evenly coated in a glossy brown sauce, with visible flecks of scrambled egg, shredded cabbage, and carrot throughout -- served in a shallow bowl or plate, ideally with visible steam and a slightly charred, griddle-fried texture rather than looking boiled or soft.

Suggested search terms: "kottu roti street food", "sri lanka kottu stir fry", "chopped roti egg vegetable"
