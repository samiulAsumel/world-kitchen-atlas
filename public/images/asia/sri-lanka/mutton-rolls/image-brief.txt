Mutton Rolls (Commonly written phonetically in Sinhala as මට්ටන් රෝල්ස් — a direct English loanword rendering rather than an older indigenous term, since the dish is a colonial-era-influenced bakery creation)
Sri Lanka - Snacks

A spiced minced mutton filling wrapped in a thin savory pancake, rolled into a cylinder, coated in egg and breadcrumbs, and deep-fried until crisp.

Key ingredients: ground mutton, onion, garlic and ginger, roasted curry powder, green chili, curry leaves, salt and pepper, vegetable oil

What it should actually look like: Golden-brown, cylindrical breaded and fried rolls, often shown halved or sliced to reveal a thin pancake wrapper around a dark, spiced meat filling.
