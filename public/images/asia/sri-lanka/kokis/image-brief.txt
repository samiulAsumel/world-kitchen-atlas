Kokis (කොකිස් (Kokis))
Sri Lanka - Desserts

A thin, crisp, lacy rosette-shaped fritter made by dipping a patterned mould into a rice flour and coconut milk batter, then deep-frying until golden and shatteringly crisp.

Key ingredients: rice flour, coconut milk, egg, sugar, salt, turmeric powder, coconut oil (or a neutral vegetable oil)

What it should actually look like: Pale golden, intricate lacy rosette or flower-shaped crisp fritters, thin and delicate looking, often stacked -- the distinctive patterned shape is the key identifier.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan kokis", "kokis rosette cookie", "sri lanka festive fried snack"
