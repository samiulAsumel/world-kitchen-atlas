Achcharu (අච්චාරු (Achcharu))
Sri Lanka - Sides

A tangy, spicy-sweet mixed vegetable pickle made with mustard seeds, chili, vinegar, and sugar, served in small amounts as a condiment.

Key ingredients: carrot, cauliflower, green beans, pearl onions, mustard seeds, dried red chili flakes, garlic, ginger

What it should actually look like: A small bowl of mixed vegetable pieces (carrot, cauliflower, beans, onion) coated in a dark, glossy, reddish-brown spiced sauce -- a chunky pickle, not a smooth chutney.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan achcharu pickle", "mixed vegetable pickle sri lanka", "sri lanka mustard pickle"
