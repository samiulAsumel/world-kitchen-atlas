Dhal Curry (Parippu) (පරිප්පු)
Sri Lanka - Sides

Red lentils simmered with turmeric and coconut milk until creamy, finished with a sizzling tempering of mustard seeds, curry leaves, and chili — the everyday backbone of a Sri Lankan rice and curry meal.

Key ingredients: red lentils, coconut milk, mustard seeds, curry leaves

What it should actually look like: A bowl of smooth, golden-yellow creamy lentils with a visible tempering of dark mustard seeds, curry leaves, and golden fried onion sitting on top rather than stirred in.
