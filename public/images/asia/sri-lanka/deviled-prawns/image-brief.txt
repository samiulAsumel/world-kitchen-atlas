Deviled Prawns (Commonly written phonetically in Sinhala as ඉස්සන් ඩෙවල් — a direct English loanword rendering rather than an older indigenous term, since the dish is a colonial-era fusion creation)
Sri Lanka - Mains

Plump prawns tossed in a glossy, spicy-sweet sauce of onions, capsicum, garlic, ginger, and chili, finished with spring onion.

Key ingredients: large prawns, cornflour, salt and pepper, vegetable oil, onion, capsicum (bell pepper), green chilies, garlic and ginger

What it should actually look like: Glossy, orange-red prawns tossed with bell pepper and onion in a thick, shiny sauce, garnished with spring onion -- similar styling to devilled chicken but clearly prawns/shrimp shaped.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan devilled prawns", "spicy fried prawns sri lanka", "devil prawns"
