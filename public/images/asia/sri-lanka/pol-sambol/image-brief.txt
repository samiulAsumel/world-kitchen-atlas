Pol Sambol (පොල් සම්බෝල)
Sri Lanka - Sides

A coarse, fiery relish of fresh grated coconut pounded with red chili, onion, and lime, traditionally sharpened with a few flakes of dried Maldive fish — the everyday condiment that appears at nearly every Sri Lankan table.

Key ingredients: grated coconut, red chili, onion, lime

What it should actually look like: A small mound of coarse, reddish-pink shredded coconut relish in a bowl, with visible individual coconut shreds (not a smooth paste) and flecks of red chili and onion.

Suggested search terms: "pol sambol coconut relish", "sri lankan coconut sambal", "coconut chili relish"
