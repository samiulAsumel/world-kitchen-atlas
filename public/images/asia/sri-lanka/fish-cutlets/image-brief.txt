Fish Cutlets (මාළු කට්ලට් (Maalu Cutlet) — a widely used phonetic loanword rendering of the English 'cutlet,' reflecting the dish's colonial-era origin rather than an older indigenous term)
Sri Lanka - Snacks

Spiced, mashed potato and flaked fish shaped into ovals, coated in egg and breadcrumbs, and deep-fried until golden and crisp.

Key ingredients: potatoes, cooked fish (tuna or mackerel), onion, green chili, curry leaves, roasted curry powder, salt and pepper, plain flour

What it should actually look like: Small, golden-brown, oval-shaped breaded and fried croquettes, crisp exterior, often shown halved to reveal a pale, flaked interior.
