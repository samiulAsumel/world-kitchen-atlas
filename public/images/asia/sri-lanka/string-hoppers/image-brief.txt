String Hoppers (ඉඳි ආප්ප)
Sri Lanka - Breads

Delicate, woven nests of steamed rice-flour noodles, pressed by hand into small discs and stacked in a steamer until tender — a lighter alternative to rice for soaking up curry.

Key ingredients: rice flour, water

What it should actually look like: A stack of two or three flat, round, white woven-noodle discs with a visible spiral/basket-weave pattern; matte white, not glossy, distinguishing it from wheat noodles.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan string hoppers", "idiyappam noodle nests", "indi appa"
