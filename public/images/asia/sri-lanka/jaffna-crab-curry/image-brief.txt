Jaffna Crab Curry (யாழ்ப்பாணம் நண்டு கறி (Yāḻppāṇam Naṇṭu Kaṟi) — presented here with standard Tamil transliteration; regional pronunciation and spelling can vary)
Sri Lanka - Mains

Whole crab simmered in a fiery, aromatic Jaffna-style coconut milk curry built on roasted curry powder, tamarind, and a heavy hand of chili.

Key ingredients: whole crab, Jaffna curry powder (dark roasted), coconut milk, tamarind pulp, onion, tomato, garlic and ginger, mustard seeds

What it should actually look like: A whole crab, shell bright orange-red, in a thick, dark reddish-brown curry sauce in a bowl or pot -- clearly a whole crab dish, not crab meat alone or a pale curry.

Suggested search terms: "jaffna crab curry", "sri lankan crab curry", "sri lanka spicy crab curry"
