Arrack (Coconut Spirit) (අරක්කු)
Sri Lanka - Drinks (Adults Only - Alcoholic)

Sri Lanka's traditional coconut-palm spirit, distilled from fermented toddy and often aged in halmilla wood -- most commonly served long over ice with ginger ale or soda and a squeeze of lime.

Key ingredients: coconut arrack, ginger ale, fresh lime juice, ice

What it should actually look like: A tall highball glass filled with ice, a pale amber, lightly fizzing mixed drink, garnished with a lime wedge on the rim -- a clean bar-style presentation rather than a shot glass, no visible branding or a real person in frame.
