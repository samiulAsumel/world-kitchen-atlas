Love Cake (ලව් කේක්)
Sri Lanka - Desserts

A dense, moist, richly spiced semolina-and-cashew cake with roots in Sri Lanka's Dutch-Portuguese Burgher community, traditionally baked for Christmas and cut into small, rich pieces.

Key ingredients: semolina, cashew nuts, butter, eggs, honey, rosewater, cardamom, nutmeg, lime zest

What it should actually look like: Small, dense, deep golden-brown to caramel-colored squares or diamonds with a moist, fudgy, slightly glossy cut surface (not a fluffy sponge crumb) -- often dusted lightly or plain, showing tiny flecks of ground cashew throughout, arranged neatly on a plate.

Suggested search terms: "sri lankan love cake", "semolina cashew cake sliced", "burgher love cake christmas"
