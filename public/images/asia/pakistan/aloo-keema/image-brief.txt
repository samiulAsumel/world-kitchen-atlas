Aloo Keema (آلو قیمہ)
Pakistan - Mains

Minced meat and diced potatoes cooked together in a spiced onion-tomato masala until both are tender -- a quicker, weeknight-friendly relative of aloo gosht that comes together in under an hour.

Key ingredients: neutral cooking oil, onion, finely chopped, ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, chopped, red chili powder, turmeric powder, coriander powder, cumin powder

What it should actually look like: A thick, reddish-brown minced meat and diced potato curry in a bowl, small potato cubes and fine ground meat visible throughout, sometimes flecked with green peas, garnished with coriander.

Suggested search terms: "aloo keema curry", "minced meat potato curry", "keema aloo pakistani"
