Halwa Puri (حلوہ پوری)
Pakistan - Desserts

Sweet semolina halwa roasted in ghee and sweetened with a saffron syrup, served alongside puffed, deep-fried whole-wheat puri bread -- Pakistan's classic weekend breakfast pairing.

Key ingredients: semolina (sooji), ghee, sugar, water, green cardamom powder, saffron strands, almonds and pistachios, slivered, whole wheat flour

What it should actually look like: A plate with a mound of glossy, deep orange-yellow semolina halwa topped with nuts, next to two or three puffed, golden-brown fried puri breads -- often with a small bowl of chickpea curry alongside.

Suggested search terms: "halwa puri breakfast", "sooji halwa with puri", "pakistani breakfast halwa puri channa"
