Paya (trotter soup) (پائے)
Pakistan - Soups

Goat or beef trotters simmered for hours until the gravy turns rich and gelatinous, spiced with ginger, garlic, and warm whole spices and finished with a hot ghee-and-chili tarka -- nihari's slow-cooked companion dish.

Key ingredients: goat or beef trotters, cleaned and split, neutral cooking oil, onion, sliced, bay leaf, black cardamom, cinnamon stick, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric powder

What it should actually look like: A deep reddish-brown, glossy, gelatinous soup in a bowl with visible trotter pieces (skin and bone), garnished with ginger julienne and green chili -- thinner and more broth-like than nihari, with a distinct sticky sheen.

Suggested search terms: "paya soup pakistani", "trotter curry", "siri paya bowl"
