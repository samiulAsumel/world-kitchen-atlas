Malai Boti (ملائی بوٹی)
Pakistan - Mains

Boneless chicken chunks marinated in cream, yogurt, and grated cheese with ginger-garlic and green chili, then skewered and char-grilled until lightly smoky -- a milder, richer cousin of chicken tikka on the same barbecue spread.

Key ingredients: boneless, skinless chicken thigh, thick plain yogurt, heavy cream, processed cheese, grated, ginger-garlic paste, green chili paste, lemon juice, garam masala

What it should actually look like: Pale, creamy-white grilled chicken chunks on a skewer with only light golden char spots -- noticeably paler than red chicken boti or tikka on the same platter, no deep red marinade color.

Suggested search terms: "malai boti kebab", "creamy chicken skewer pakistani", "white marinated grilled chicken"
