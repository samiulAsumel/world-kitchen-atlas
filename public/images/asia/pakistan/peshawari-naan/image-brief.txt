Peshawari Naan (پشاوری نان)
Pakistan - Breads

Leavened tandoor bread stuffed with a sweet filling of coconut, dried fruit, and nuts, then baked until puffed and lightly charred -- Peshawar's distinctive, subtly sweet take on naan.

Key ingredients: all-purpose flour, instant yeast, sugar, warm milk, plain yogurt, ghee, salt, warm water

What it should actually look like: A puffed, golden-brown tandoor naan with visible flecks of coconut and dried fruit filling peeking through small tears in the surface -- distinctly different from plain naan's smooth, unfilled surface.

Suggested search terms: "peshawari naan", "stuffed sweet naan pakistani", "coconut nut naan bread"
