Multani Sohan Halwa (ملتانی سوہن حلوہ)
Pakistan - Desserts

Multan's famous version of sohan halwa -- a cornflour, milk, and ghee mixture cooked far longer than usual and pressed into thin, brittle, deep-amber discs generously topped with pistachios, sold in decorative tins as a signature gift sweet.

Key ingredients: cornflour (cornstarch), whole milk, sugar, water, saffron strands, ghee, pistachios, finely chopped, almonds, finely slivered

What it should actually look like: Thin, flat, brittle amber-gold discs or shards densely studded with chopped pistachios, often shown stacked or in a decorative tin -- visibly thinner and drier-looking than regular sohan halwa, with a glassy, cracked surface.

Suggested search terms: "multani sohan halwa", "multan sweet tin", "sohan halwa multan pistachio"
