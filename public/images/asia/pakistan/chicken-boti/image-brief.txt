Chicken Boti (چکن بوٹی)
Pakistan - Mains

Boneless chicken chunks marinated in a spicy yogurt, chili, and ginger-garlic mixture, then skewered and char-grilled until charred and juicy -- the spicier counterpart to milder cream-based kebabs on a Pakistani BBQ platter.

Key ingredients: boneless, skinless chicken thigh, thick plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, lemon juice, cumin powder, coriander powder, garam masala

What it should actually look like: Deep red-orange grilled chicken chunks on a skewer with pronounced dark char marks -- visibly spicier and redder than the pale, creamy malai boti on the same grill platter.

Suggested search terms: "chicken boti kebab", "spicy grilled chicken skewer pakistani", "red chicken boti bbq"
