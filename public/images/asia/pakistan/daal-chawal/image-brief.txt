Daal Chawal (دال چاول)
Pakistan - Mains

Split lentils simmered until soft and finished with a garlic-and-cumin ghee tempering, served spooned over steamed basmati rice -- the simplest, most everyday comfort meal in Pakistani home cooking.

Key ingredients: chana daal (split chickpea lentils), rinsed, water, turmeric powder, salt, ghee, cumin seeds, garlic cloves, sliced, onion, sliced

What it should actually look like: A thick yellow-brown lentil curry with a golden ghee tempering (visible garlic and cumin flecks) served beside or over plain white steamed rice -- simple, no meat, no thick gravy.

Suggested search terms: "daal chawal", "lentil curry rice pakistani", "chana daal tadka rice"
