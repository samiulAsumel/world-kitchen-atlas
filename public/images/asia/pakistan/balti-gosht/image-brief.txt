Balti Gosht (بالٹی گوشت)
Pakistan - Mains

Meat and vegetables quickly stir-fried and simmered together in a flat-bottomed cast-iron pot until the gravy clings to every piece -- a fast-cooking curry whose exact origin is genuinely debated between Baltistan and the Pakistani restaurant scene of 1970s Birmingham.

Key ingredients: neutral cooking oil, boneless mutton or chicken, cubed, onion, sliced, ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, chopped, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder

What it should actually look like: A dark, glossy meat curry with visible strips of green and red bell pepper still bright and slightly crisp, served sizzling in a flat-bottomed cast-iron balti pot rather than a bowl.

Suggested search terms: "balti gosht", "balti curry cast iron pot", "pakistani balti pepper curry"
