Biryani (Karachi style) (کراچی بریانی)
Pakistan - Mains

Bone-in meat cooked into a dark, tomato-forward masala with potato and a few whole dried plums for tang, then layered with parboiled rice and slow-steamed until fully infused -- Karachi's spicier, more intensely flavored take on biryani.

Key ingredients: bone-in chicken or beef, cubed, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, neutral cooking oil or ghee, onion, thinly sliced

What it should actually look like: Deep reddish-orange layered rice with visible whole dried plums (aloo bukhara), potato chunks, and bone-in meat, streaked with saffron color and topped with crispy fried onions -- darker and spicier-looking than paler Sindhi biryani.

Suggested search terms: "karachi biryani", "sindhi meat biryani with potato", "pakistani biryani aloo bukhara"
