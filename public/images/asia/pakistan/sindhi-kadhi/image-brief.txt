Sindhi Kadhi (سندھی کڑھی)
Pakistan - Mains

Roasted gram flour simmered into a tangy, tamarind-soured curry loaded with okra, drumstick, potato, and eggplant -- Sindh's distinct, dairy-free take on kadhi, traditionally eaten with plain rice.

Key ingredients: gram flour (besan), neutral cooking oil, cumin seeds, mustard seeds, fenugreek seeds, curry leaves, onion, sliced, ginger-garlic paste

What it should actually look like: A tangy, golden-brown gravy loaded with visible okra, drumstick pieces, potato, and eggplant chunks -- no fried dumplings floating in it (that's Punjabi kadhi), served over or beside plain rice.

Suggested search terms: "sindhi kadhi", "sindhi vegetable curry", "besan kadhi with vegetables"
