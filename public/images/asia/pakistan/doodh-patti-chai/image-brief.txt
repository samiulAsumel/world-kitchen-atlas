Doodh Patti Chai (دودھ پتی چائے)
Pakistan - Drinks

Black tea leaves boiled directly in milk, rather than steeped separately, until strong, richly colored, and creamy -- Pakistan's everyday cup of chai, served hot with sugar.

Key ingredients: water, green cardamom pods, crushed, black CTC tea leaves, loose, whole milk, sugar

What it should actually look like: A cup of reddish-brown, opaque milk tea with a slight creamy sheen on top -- noticeably darker and more opaque than pale British-style milk tea, served hot in a small cup or glass.

Suggested search terms: "doodh patti chai", "pakistani milk tea cup", "karak chai desi"
