Sohan Halwa (سوہن حلوہ)
Pakistan - Desserts

A dense, chewy halwa made by slowly working ghee into a cooked cornflour, milk, and sugar base until it turns glossy and jelly-like, set in a tray, garnished with nuts, and cut into diamonds -- a labor-intensive festive sweet.

Key ingredients: cornflour (cornstarch), whole milk, sugar, water, saffron strands, green cardamom powder, ghee, almonds, slivered

What it should actually look like: Small diamond- or square-cut pieces of a dense, glossy, amber-gold sweet with visible nut pieces pressed into the surface -- chewy-looking rather than crumbly, arranged neatly on a plate.

Suggested search terms: "sohan halwa", "pakistani cornstarch halwa sweet", "punjabi sohan halwa pieces"
