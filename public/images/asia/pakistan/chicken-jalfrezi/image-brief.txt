Chicken Jalfrezi (چکن جلفریزی)
Pakistan - Mains

Boneless chicken stir-fried with bell peppers, onions, and green chilies in a thick, tangy tomato-chili sauce -- a quick-cooking, restaurant-favorite curry with roots in Anglo-Indian colonial-era cooking.

Key ingredients: boneless chicken breast or thigh, sliced, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, neutral cooking oil, onion, sliced into strips, garlic, sliced

What it should actually look like: A thick, glossy red-orange sauce clinging tightly to chicken pieces and bright, still-crisp strips of red, green, and yellow bell pepper -- visibly drier and chunkier than a soupy curry.

Suggested search terms: "chicken jalfrezi", "bell pepper chicken curry", "pakistani jalfrezi stir fry curry"
