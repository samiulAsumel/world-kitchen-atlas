Kheer (Pakistani style) (کھیر)
Pakistan - Desserts

Rice slow-simmered in milk until it breaks down into a smooth, thickened pudding, sweetened and scented with cardamom and saffron, and topped with slivered nuts -- a festive milk dessert found across Pakistani households.

Key ingredients: rice, rinsed and soaked, whole milk, sugar, green cardamom powder, saffron strands, almonds, slivered, pistachios, slivered, rose water

What it should actually look like: A smooth, creamy-white to pale ivory rice pudding in a bowl, no distinct rice grains visible (fully broken down), topped with slivered almonds and pistachios and a few golden saffron threads.

Suggested search terms: "kheer pakistani rice pudding", "chawal ki kheer", "pakistani kheer dessert bowl"
