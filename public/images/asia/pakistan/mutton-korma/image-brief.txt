Mutton Korma (مٹن قورمہ)
Pakistan - Mains

Bone-in mutton slow-cooked in a rich fried-onion and yogurt masala, enriched with nut paste and warm whole spices, until the meat is fall-apart tender and a glossy layer of oil rises to the surface -- a festive Mughlai-heritage curry.

Key ingredients: onion, thinly sliced, neutral cooking oil, ghee, bay leaf, black cardamom, green cardamom, cloves, cinnamon stick

What it should actually look like: A thick, deep golden-brown curry with a distinct glossy layer of oil visible on the surface, bone-in meat pieces mostly submerged in a rich, smooth gravy flecked with fried onion.

Suggested search terms: "mutton korma", "pakistani korma curry oil layer", "mughlai mutton korma bowl"
