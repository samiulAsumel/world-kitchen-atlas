Sugarcane Juice (Sherbet-e-Ghous) (گنے کا رس)
Pakistan - Drinks

Fresh sugarcane stalks pressed with ginger, strained, and finished with lemon juice and mint -- a naturally sweet, cooling street drink sold across Pakistan, especially in summer.

Key ingredients: fresh sugarcane stalks, peeled, ginger, peeled, lemon or lime juice, mint leaves, black salt, ice cubes

What it should actually look like: A tall glass of pale greenish-yellow juice over ice, garnished with a mint sprig and lemon wedge, often shown next to a roadside roller-press cart with raw sugarcane stalks visible.

Suggested search terms: "sugarcane juice glass", "ganne ka ras", "fresh sugarcane juice street vendor"
