White Karahi (سفید کڑاہی)
Pakistan - Mains

Bone-in chicken cooked in a karahi with yogurt, cream, garlic, and whole green chilies, deliberately made without tomato or red chili powder for a pale, rich gravy seasoned with white pepper -- a Peshawari specialty distinct from standard red karahi.

Key ingredients: bone-in chicken, cubed, ghee or neutral cooking oil, garlic, sliced, ginger, julienned, green chilies, whole, plain yogurt, heavy cream, white pepper powder

What it should actually look like: A pale, creamy-white to ivory chicken curry in a karahi, glossy from cream, topped with whole green chilies and ginger julienne -- distinctly pale rather than the deep red of standard tomato-based karahi.

Suggested search terms: "white karahi", "peshawari safed karahi", "cream chicken karahi pakistani"
