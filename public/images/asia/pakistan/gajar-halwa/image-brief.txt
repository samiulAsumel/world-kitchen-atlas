Gajar Halwa (گاجر کا حلوہ)
Pakistan - Desserts

Grated red carrots slow-cooked in milk until soft, then finished with sugar, ghee, and cardamom and garnished with nuts -- a warm, richly aromatic winter dessert found across Pakistani Punjab.

Key ingredients: red carrots (or orange carrots), grated, whole milk, sugar, ghee, green cardamom powder, almonds, slivered, pistachios, slivered, raisins

What it should actually look like: A moist, deep orange-red dessert mounded in a bowl or plate, glossy from ghee, topped with slivered almonds, pistachios, and raisins -- no liquid pooling, a soft spoonable texture.

Suggested search terms: "gajar halwa", "carrot halwa dessert", "pakistani carrot pudding"
