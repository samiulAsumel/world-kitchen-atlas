Aloo Gosht (آلو گوشت)
Pakistan - Mains

Bone-in mutton or beef simmered with potatoes in a spiced onion-tomato gravy until both the meat and potatoes are fork-tender -- one of the most common home-cooked curries in Pakistani kitchens.

Key ingredients: neutral cooking oil, onion, sliced, bay leaf, cinnamon stick, black cardamom, ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, chopped, plain yogurt

What it should actually look like: A reddish-brown meat-and-potato curry in a bowl, large potato chunks and bone-in meat visible in a moderately thick gravy, garnished with coriander and ginger julienne.

Suggested search terms: "aloo gosht curry", "potato meat curry pakistani", "mutton potato curry bowl"
