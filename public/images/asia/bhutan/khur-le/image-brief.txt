Khur-le (ཁུར་ལེ།)
Bhutan - Breads

A simple buckwheat pancake cooked on a flat griddle, lightly crisp at the edges -- an everyday breakfast staple in Bhutan's Haa Valley.

Key ingredients: buckwheat flour, water, salt, vegetable oil, for the griddle

What it should actually look like: A stack of thin, round, greyish-brown buckwheat pancakes with lightly browned, speckled edges -- flatter and more crepe-like than a thick wheat pancake.

Suggested search terms: "Khur-le Bhutanese food", "Khur-le recipe Bhutan", "traditional Bhutanese breads"
