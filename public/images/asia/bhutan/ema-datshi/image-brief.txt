Ema Datshi (ཨེ་མ་དར་ཚི།)
Bhutan - Mains

Bhutan's national dish -- whole green and red chilies simmered until tender in a melted local cheese sauce, eaten as the main vegetable dish alongside red rice.

Key ingredients: green chilies, sliced lengthwise, red chilies, sliced lengthwise, onion, sliced, tomato, chopped, garlic, sliced, water, datshi (Bhutanese cheese), crumbled or sliced thin, vegetable oil or butter, salt

What it should actually look like: A small bowl of whole green and red chili strips coated in a thick, pale, melted-cheese sauce -- the chilies should still be visibly whole/sliced, not chopped small, distinguishing it from a generic chili sauce.

Suggested search terms: "Ema Datshi Bhutanese food", "Ema Datshi recipe Bhutan", "traditional Bhutanese mains"
