Nakey (ནགས་ཀྱེ།)
Bhutan - Sides

Foraged fiddlehead ferns stir-fried with onion, garlic, and chili -- a brief, seasonal spring specialty from Bhutan's forested hillsides.

Key ingredients: fiddlehead ferns, trimmed and rinsed, vegetable oil, onion, sliced, garlic, minced, green chili, sliced, tomato, chopped, salt

What it should actually look like: A bowl of coiled, bright green fiddlehead fern spirals stir-fried with onion and chili -- the distinctive tight spiral/coil shape of each fern tip is the key identifying visual.
