Zow Shungo (ཟོས་ཤིང་འོག)
Bhutan - Mains

Leftover cooked red rice stir-fried with vegetables and light seasoning -- a practical, everyday way of using up rice from a previous meal.

Key ingredients: leftover cooked Bhutanese red rice, vegetable oil, onion, chopped, mixed vegetables (cabbage, carrot, spinach), chopped, garlic, minced, green chili, chopped, salt

What it should actually look like: A plate of reddish-brown rice grains stir-fried with visible small pieces of colorful vegetables (carrot, cabbage, spinach) -- looks like a simple fried rice with distinct rice grains, not mushy.
