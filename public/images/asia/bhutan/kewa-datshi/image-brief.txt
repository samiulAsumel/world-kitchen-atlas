Kewa Datshi (ཀེ་ཝ་དར་ཚི།)
Bhutan - Mains

Tender potato slices simmered and finished with melted local cheese into a mild, creamy stew -- a comforting, everyday counterpart to the fierier ema datshi.

Key ingredients: potato, peeled and sliced, onion, sliced, green chili, sliced, tomato, chopped, water, datshi (Bhutanese cheese), crumbled or sliced thin, vegetable oil or butter, salt

What it should actually look like: A bowl of pale potato slices coated in a thick, creamy white-yellow cheese sauce, flecked with a little onion and chili -- milder and paler-looking than ema datshi's red-green chili dish.
