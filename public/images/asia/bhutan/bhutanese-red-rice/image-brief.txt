Bhutanese Red Rice (འབྲས་དམར།)
Bhutan - Sides

Bhutan's staple grain -- a semi-milled, reddish-husked rice with a nutty flavor and soft, slightly sticky texture, the everyday base for the country's chili- and cheese-forward main dishes.

Key ingredients: Bhutanese red rice, rinsed, water, salt

What it should actually look like: A mound of reddish-brown rice grains, distinctly tinted red-brown rather than white or plain brown, with a slightly nutty, matte appearance, served plain on a plate.
