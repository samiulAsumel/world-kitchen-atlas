Suja (སུཇ།)
Bhutan - Drinks

Bhutan's traditional butter tea -- strong black tea churned with butter and salt into a rich, savory drink, valued for its warming character in the country's high-altitude climate.

Key ingredients: water, black tea leaves (strong, such as CTC), butter, at room temperature, salt

What it should actually look like: A cup of pale, opaque, tan-colored frothy tea with a thin layer of oily sheen on top -- distinctly paler and more opaque than black tea, with a faint separated-oil look at the rim.
