Phaksha Paa (ཕག་ཤ་ར་དོག)
Bhutan - Mains

Pork simmered with radish and a generous quantity of dried red chili into a rich, warming stew -- a hearty, cold-weather favorite alongside red rice.

Key ingredients: pork belly or shoulder, cut into bite-sized pieces, radish, peeled and cut into thick pieces, dried red chili, rehydrated and roughly chopped, garlic, sliced, ginger, sliced, onion, sliced, water, salt

What it should actually look like: A bowl of pork pieces and pale translucent radish chunks in a deep reddish-brown reduced sauce, flecked with visible dried chili pieces -- the fattier pork pieces should look glossy.
