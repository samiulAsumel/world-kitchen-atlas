Jasha Maru (བྱ་ཤ་དམར།)
Bhutan - Mains

Shredded or minced chicken cooked down with onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, and a generous amount of chili into a thick, richly spiced stew.

Key ingredients: boneless chicken thigh, shredded or finely chopped, vegetable oil, onion, chopped, garlic, minced, ginger, minced, tomato, chopped, green chili, sliced, red chili powder, water, salt, cilantro, chopped

What it should actually look like: A bowl of shredded, deep red-brown chicken in a thick, reduced tomato-chili sauce -- the meat should look shredded/torn rather than in large chunks, densely coated in sauce.

Suggested search terms: "Jasha Maru Bhutanese food", "Jasha Maru recipe Bhutan", "traditional Bhutanese mains"
