Yak Meat Stew (འབྲོང་ཤ་ཁོག)
Bhutan - Mains

Yak meat slow-simmered with radish, chili, and aromatics into a hearty, warming stew from Bhutan's high-altitude herding communities.

Key ingredients: yak meat, cut into chunks, vegetable oil, onion, chopped, garlic, sliced, ginger, sliced, radish, peeled and cut into thick pieces, dried red chili, rehydrated and chopped, water, salt

What it should actually look like: A bowl of dark, tender meat chunks and pale radish pieces in a deep reddish-brown reduced sauce -- similar to phaksha paa but with leaner, darker red meat rather than pale pork.
