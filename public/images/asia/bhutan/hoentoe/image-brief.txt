Hoentoe (ཧྭན་ཏོག)
Bhutan - Snacks

A buckwheat-flour dumpling stuffed with turnip greens, local cheese, and spices, steamed and traditionally associated with Bhutan's Haa Valley and the autumn harvest.

Key ingredients: buckwheat flour, warm water, salt, turnip greens, blanched and finely chopped, datshi (Bhutanese cheese) or a soft farmer's cheese, crumbled, onion, finely chopped, green chili, finely chopped, vegetable oil or butter, salt

What it should actually look like: Small, pale grey-brown dumpling pouches with a slightly rustic, hand-folded pleated top, arranged on a plate -- denser and less translucent-looking than a wheat-flour momo.

Suggested search terms: "Hoentoe Bhutanese food", "Hoentoe recipe Bhutan", "traditional Bhutanese snacks"
