Khabzey (ཁ་བཟས།)
Bhutan - Desserts

A crisp, twisted, deep-fried sweet biscuit, traditionally made in large batches for Losar (Bhutanese New Year) and shared with visiting guests.

Key ingredients: all-purpose flour, sugar, butter, melted, egg, water, salt, vegetable oil, for deep-frying

What it should actually look like: A pile of small, golden-brown, twisted or bow-shaped crispy fried biscuits with a slightly bumpy, ridged surface, stacked in a basket -- unmistakably twisted/braided in shape.

Suggested search terms: "Khabzey Bhutanese food", "Khabzey recipe Bhutan", "traditional Bhutanese desserts"
