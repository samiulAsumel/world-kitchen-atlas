Shamu Datshi (ཤ་མུ་དར་ཚི།)
Bhutan - Mains

Sliced mushrooms simmered and finished with melted local cheese into an earthy, creamy stew -- a mild, forest-flavored member of Bhutan's datshi family.

Key ingredients: mushrooms, sliced, onion, sliced, green chili, sliced, tomato, chopped, water, datshi (Bhutanese cheese), crumbled or sliced thin, vegetable oil or butter, salt

What it should actually look like: A bowl of sliced mushrooms in a thick, pale cheese sauce, deeper brown-grey in tone than kewa datshi due to the mushrooms, with visible mushroom slices rather than chili or potato.
