Bhutan - Desserts

A golden, sweet rice dish enriched with butter, sugar, and dried fruit, traditionally colored with saffron or turmeric and served at festivals and special occasions.

Key ingredients: Bhutanese red rice or white rice, rinsed, water, saffron threads, butter, sugar, raisins, cashews, roughly chopped

What it should actually look like: A mound of glossy, golden-yellow rice studded with dark raisins and pale chopped cashews -- the vivid saffron-yellow color and visible dried fruit distinguish it from plain rice.
