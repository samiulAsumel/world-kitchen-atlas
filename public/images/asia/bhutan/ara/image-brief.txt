Ara (ཨ་ར།)
Bhutan - Drinks

A traditional Bhutanese distilled spirit made from fermented rice, maize, or wheat -- often served warm, sometimes with a fried egg or butter stirred in.

Key ingredients: rice, maize, or wheat, water, for cooking the grain, traditional fermentation starter, crushed, water, for distillation and dilution

What it should actually look like: A small clear glass or traditional cup filled with a completely clear, colorless liquid, sometimes with a fried egg visible in the cup -- no cloudiness or color, resembling a clear spirit.

Suggested search terms: "Ara Bhutanese food", "Ara recipe Bhutan", "traditional Bhutanese drinks"
