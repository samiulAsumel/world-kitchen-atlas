Ngo-ah Paa (ང་འོག་ཕག)
Bhutan - Mains

Fish simmered with onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, and a generous amount of chili into a bold, richly spiced curry, served alongside red rice.

Key ingredients: firm white fish fillet, cut into pieces, vegetable oil, onion, chopped, garlic, minced, ginger, minced, tomato, chopped, green chili, sliced, red chili powder, water, salt, cilantro, chopped

What it should actually look like: A bowl of white fish pieces in a thick, deep red chili-tomato sauce, garnished with cilantro -- the fish should look intact in chunks, not flaked apart, coated in a glossy red sauce.
