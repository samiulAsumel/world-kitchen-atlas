Momos (Bhutanese Style) (མོག་མོག)
Bhutan - Snacks

Steamed dumplings filled with spiced minced meat and datshi cheese, a Bhutanese take on the wider Himalayan momo tradition, served with a fiery ezay chili condiment.

Key ingredients: all-purpose flour, water, salt, minced beef or pork, datshi (Bhutanese cheese), crumbled, onion, very finely chopped, garlic, minced, ginger, minced, cilantro, chopped, vegetable oil, salt, green chili, chopped, tomato, chopped, datshi (Bhutanese cheese), crumbled, salt

What it should actually look like: A plate of plump, pale, hand-pleated dumplings arranged in a circle, served beside a small bowl of chunky, vivid red-green ezay chili relish -- the coarse, chunky red condiment distinguishes it from a smooth dip.
