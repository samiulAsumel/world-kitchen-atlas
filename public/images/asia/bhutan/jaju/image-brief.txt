Jaju (ཇ་ཇུ།)
Bhutan - Soups

A simple, mild soup of leafy greens simmered in milk, offering a gentle, comforting counterpart to Bhutan's more intensely spiced chili and cheese dishes.

Key ingredients: spinach, washed and roughly chopped, whole milk, water, garlic, minced, butter or vegetable oil, salt

What it should actually look like: A pale, soft green soup bowl with wilted spinach visible in a milky, creamy-white broth -- distinctly milky and pale compared to a clear vegetable soup.
