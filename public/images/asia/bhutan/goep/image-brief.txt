Goep (སྒོག་པ།)
Bhutan - Mains

Tripe simmered with dried chili, radish, and aromatics into a rich, warming stew -- a well-loved, if less internationally known, Bhutanese meat dish.

Key ingredients: tripe, thoroughly cleaned and cut into strips, water, for pre-boiling, radish, peeled and cut into thick pieces, dried red chili, rehydrated and roughly chopped, garlic, sliced, ginger, sliced, onion, sliced, water, for the stew, salt

What it should actually look like: A bowl of chewy, pale tan-grey tripe strips and pale radish chunks in a deep reddish-brown chili sauce -- the tripe's honeycomb or ridged texture should be visible on close pieces.

Suggested search terms: "Goep Bhutanese food", "Goep recipe Bhutan", "traditional Bhutanese mains"
