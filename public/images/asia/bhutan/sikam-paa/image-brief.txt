Sikam Paa (སྲི་ཁམ་ཕག)
Bhutan - Snacks

Dried, air-cured pork, rehydrated and stir-fried with chili and radish into a concentrated, salty, savory dish -- a traditional way of preserving meat through Bhutan's winter.

Key ingredients: dried pork (sikam), soaked and thinly sliced, vegetable oil, garlic, sliced, ginger, sliced, radish, cut into thin strips, dried red chili, rehydrated and chopped, onion, sliced, water, salt

What it should actually look like: A bowl of dark, chewy dried pork strips stir-fried with pale radish strips, glazed and speckled with chili -- the meat should look darker and drier/denser than fresh pork.
