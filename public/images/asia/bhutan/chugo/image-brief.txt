Chugo (ཕྱུ་གོ།)
Bhutan - Snacks

An extremely hard, dried yak cheese, chewed slowly over a long time like a chewing gum -- a portable, long-lasting snack from Bhutan's high-altitude herding communities.

Key ingredients: whole yak milk, lemon juice or vinegar, for curdling, salt

What it should actually look like: Small, hard, pale ivory-tan cubes or rounds of dried cheese, matte and rock-hard looking, strung or piled together -- should look distinctly hard and dry, not soft or creamy like fresh cheese.

Suggested search terms: "Chugo Bhutanese food", "Chugo recipe Bhutan", "traditional Bhutanese snacks"
