Puta (པུ་ཏ།)
Bhutan - Mains

Buckwheat noodles pressed directly into boiling water, then tossed with butter and simple seasonings -- a regional specialty of Bhutan's Haa Valley.

Key ingredients: buckwheat flour, warm water, salt, butter, green chili, sliced, toasted sesame seeds

What it should actually look like: A bowl of short, greyish-brown buckwheat noodles glistening with melted butter, with a slightly rougher, more matte surface than glossy wheat noodles.

Suggested search terms: "Puta Bhutanese food", "Puta recipe Bhutan", "traditional Bhutanese mains"
