Tongba (तोङ्बा)
Nepal - Drinks

A warm, mildly alcoholic fermented millet drink from eastern Nepal, sipped through a bamboo straw directly from the vessel and refilled with hot water as you drink.

Key ingredients: finger millet, water, for cooking the millet, marcha (traditional fermentation starter), crushed, hot water, for steeping and refilling

What it should actually look like: A tall wooden or bamboo vessel filled with pale, swollen fermented millet grains submerged in cloudy, pale liquid, with a bamboo straw sticking out of the top -- unmistakable due to the straw and the visible grain filling the vessel.

Suggested search terms: "Tongba Nepali food", "Tongba recipe Nepal", "traditional Nepali drinks"
