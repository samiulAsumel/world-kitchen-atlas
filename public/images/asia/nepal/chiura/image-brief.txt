Chiura (चिउरा)
Nepal - Snacks

Beaten, flattened rice served the classic way -- softened with warm milk or yogurt and a little sugar, or tossed savory with onion, chili, and mustard oil.

Key ingredients: chiura (beaten rice), warm whole milk, sugar, banana, sliced, chiura (beaten rice), mustard oil, onion, finely chopped, green chili, finely chopped, salt, lemon juice

What it should actually look like: A bowl of pale, flattened, slightly translucent rice flakes, either creamy-white and glistening when soaked in milk, or golden-flecked with mustard oil, onion, and chili in the savory version -- distinctly flatter and drier-looking than cooked rice.
