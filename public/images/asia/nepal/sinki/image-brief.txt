Sinki (सिन्की)
Nepal - Sides

A tangy, sour soup made from sinki -- sun-dried, pit-fermented radish -- simmered with garlic, chili, and tomato, eaten as a side alongside rice and dal through the Nepali winter.

Key ingredients: dried sinki (fermented radish), soaked and roughly chopped, mustard oil, garlic, minced, ginger, minced, dried red chili, whole, tomato, chopped, turmeric powder, fenugreek seeds, water, salt

What it should actually look like: A small bowl of thin, pale-golden sour soup with tangled, sinewy dark strands of fermented radish suspended in it -- visibly stringier and more root-like than gundruk's leafy tangle, with flecks of red chili and garlic in the broth.
