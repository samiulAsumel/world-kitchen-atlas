Samay Baji (समय बजी)
Nepal - Mains

A traditional Newari feast platter built around beaten rice, combining spiced grilled meat, boiled egg, roasted soybeans, potato salad, and other small dishes on one plate.

Key ingredients: chiura (beaten rice), boneless chicken thigh, cut into small pieces, mustard oil, garlic, minced, ginger, minced, timur (Sichuan pepper) or black pepper, ground, red chili powder, green chili, chopped, salt, eggs, dried soybeans, soaked overnight and boiled, potato, boiled and cubed, mustard oil, onion, finely chopped, lemon juice, salt

What it should actually look like: A large plate with distinct sections: a mound of pale beaten rice, dark charred pieces of grilled meat, a halved boiled egg, a pile of roasted soybeans, and a small mound of potato salad, all kept visually separate rather than mixed.
