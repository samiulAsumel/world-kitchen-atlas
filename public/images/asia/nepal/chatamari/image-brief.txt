Chatamari (चतामरी (Chatamari))
Nepal - Breads

A thin, savory rice-flour crepe from the Kathmandu Valley's Newari cuisine, cooked on a hot griddle and topped with minced meat, egg, and spring onion -- often nicknamed "Newari pizza."

Key ingredients: rice flour, water, salt, minced buffalo or goat meat, egg, onion, finely chopped, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, salt, spring onion, chopped, mustard oil

What it should actually look like: A thin, round, golden-edged rice-flour crepe topped with browned minced meat and a fried egg in the center, cut into wedges -- its flat, round, pizza-like top-down appearance is the defining visual, unlike a folded or rolled crepe.

Suggested search terms: "chatamari Newari pizza", "Nepali rice crepe", "Newari rice flour pancake"
