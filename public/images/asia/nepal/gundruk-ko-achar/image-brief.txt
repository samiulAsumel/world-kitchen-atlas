Gundruk ko Achar (गुन्द्रुकको अचार (Gundruk ko Achar))
Nepal - Sides

A tangy, spiced pickle made from fermented, sun-dried gundruk greens, tempered with mustard oil, garlic, and chili and often simmered with tomato and potato -- a classic dal-bhat side.

Key ingredients: dried gundruk (fermented greens), mustard oil, potato, garlic, ginger, tomato

What it should actually look like: A brownish-green, tangy relish with visible shredded fermented greens and small potato cubes in a light, spiced sauce, served in a small side bowl -- distinct from a fresh leafy-green curry by its darker, fermented-looking greens and pickle-like appearance.

Suggested search terms: "gundruk ko achar", "nepali fermented greens pickle", "gundruk pickle nepal"
