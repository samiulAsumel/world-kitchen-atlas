Panipuri (Nepali Style) (पानीपुरी)
Nepal - Snacks

Crisp, hollow semolina shells filled with spiced potato and chickpeas, dunked in a tangy, chili-spiked tamarind water -- Nepal's street-vendor take on a beloved South Asian snack.

Key ingredients: fine semolina (sooji), all-purpose flour, water, for the dough, salt, vegetable oil, for deep-frying, potato, boiled and mashed coarsely, boiled black chickpeas or white chickpeas, chatpate spice mix (cumin, chili powder, black salt), onion, finely chopped, tamarind water, mint leaves, cilantro, green chili, black salt, cumin powder, toasted, cold water

What it should actually look like: A small round, hollow, golden-brown fried shell being filled with pale mashed potato and dark chickpeas, next to a bowl of vivid green tamarind-mint water -- the shell's visible crisp hollow interior is the key identifying detail.
