Chhoyala (छ्वयला)
Nepal - Snacks

Newari-style char-grilled duck meat, marinated in mustard oil, garlic, and ground spices, chopped and tossed with onion and fresh herbs -- a close cousin of choila built around duck instead of buffalo.

Key ingredients: boneless duck leg and breast, skin on, mustard oil, garlic, minced, ginger, minced, timur (Sichuan pepper), ground, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, onion, thinly sliced, green chili, chopped, cilantro, chopped

What it should actually look like: A plate of dark, charred, chopped duck meat pieces tossed with visible raw onion slices, green chili, and cilantro, glistening with mustard oil -- the deep char on the skin distinguishes it from a plain curry.
