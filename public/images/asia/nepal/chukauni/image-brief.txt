Chukauni (चुकौनी)
Nepal - Sides

A tangy far-western Nepali potato salad, boiled potato slices dressed in a mustard-seed and yogurt sauce tempered with fenugreek, turmeric, and dried chili.

Key ingredients: potato, boiled, peeled, and sliced, plain yogurt, mustard seed, ground, turmeric powder, mustard oil, fenugreek seeds, dried red chili, whole, garlic, minced, onion, finely chopped, salt, cilantro, chopped

What it should actually look like: A bowl of pale, creamy-yellow potato slices coated in a thin yogurt-mustard sauce, topped with a few dark mustard seeds and a scattering of cilantro -- distinctly creamier and paler than an oil-based potato pickle.
