Raksi (रक्सी)
Nepal - Drinks

A traditional Nepali distilled spirit made from fermented rice, millet, or barley -- a clear, potent drink served at festivals, gatherings, and as a gesture of hospitality.

Key ingredients: rice, millet, or barley, water, for cooking the grain, marcha (traditional fermentation starter), crushed, water, for distillation and dilution

What it should actually look like: A small clear glass or traditional cup filled with a completely clear, colorless liquid -- no cloudiness, sediment, or color, resembling a clear spirit rather than a fermented or milky drink.

Suggested search terms: "Raksi Nepali food", "Raksi recipe Nepal", "traditional Nepali drinks"
