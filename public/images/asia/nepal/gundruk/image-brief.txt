Gundruk (गुन्द्रुक (Gundruk))
Nepal - Sides

Sun-dried, naturally fermented leafy greens (usually mustard, radish, or cauliflower leaves) rehydrated and simmered with potato and tomato into a tangy, sour-forward side dish or light soup.

Key ingredients: dried fermented gundruk, potato, peeled and cubed, tomato, chopped, mustard oil, cumin seeds, garlic, minced, ginger, minced, dried red chili, turmeric powder, salt, water

What it should actually look like: A small bowl of dark green, tangled fermented greens simmered with pale potato cubes in a thin, tomato-tinted reddish sauce -- visibly different from a fresh spinach curry by its darker, slightly wilted-looking greens and cloudy broth.
