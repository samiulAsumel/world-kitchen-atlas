Sel Roti (सेल रोटी)
Nepal - Breads

A ring-shaped, deep-fried sweet rice bread, crisp outside and soft within, hand-poured into hot oil and a defining food of Nepal's Dashain and Tihar festivals.

Key ingredients: rice, soaked 4+ hours and drained, sugar, whole milk, ghee, melted, cardamom powder, water, vegetable oil, for deep-frying

What it should actually look like: A stack of golden-brown, ring-shaped fried bread with a slightly bumpy, uneven surface and a visible hole in the center -- unmistakably donut-shaped but denser and less uniform than a Western doughnut.

Suggested search terms: "Sel Roti Nepali food", "Sel Roti recipe Nepal", "traditional Nepali breads"
