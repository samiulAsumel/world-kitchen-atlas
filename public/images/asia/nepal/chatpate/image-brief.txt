Chatpate (चटपटे)
Nepal - Snacks

Puffed rice tossed with potato, peas, tomato, onion, and a sharp tamarind-lemon dressing in Nepal's signature tangy, spicy chatpate style -- a beloved street-cart snack.

Key ingredients: puffed rice, potato, boiled and diced small, boiled green peas or black chickpeas, tomato, finely chopped, onion, finely chopped, green chili, finely chopped, tamarind water, lemon juice, chatpate spice mix (cumin, chili powder, black salt), cilantro, chopped, salt

What it should actually look like: A bowl of puffed rice stained reddish-brown from tamarind and spice, mixed with visible small potato cubes, peas, chopped tomato, and onion -- looks loose and grainy, not a wet salad.
