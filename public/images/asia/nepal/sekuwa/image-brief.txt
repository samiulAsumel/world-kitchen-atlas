Sekuwa (सेकुवा (Sekuwa))
Nepal - Mains

Marinated, skewered meat -- traditionally goat -- grilled over hot charcoal or open flame for a smoky char, typically served with beaten rice and a spicy tomato achar.

Key ingredients: goat meat, cubed, mustard oil, ginger-garlic paste, timur (Nepali Sichuan pepper), ground, cumin powder, coriander powder, turmeric powder, red chili powder, salt, lemon juice, tomato, dried red chili, garlic, mustard oil, salt, chiura (beaten rice), onion, sliced

What it should actually look like: Skewers of charred, dark-edged marinated meat cubes fresh off the grill, served alongside a small mound of flattened beaten rice (chiura) and a reddish tomato achar -- the visible char marks and smoky exterior distinguish it from a saucy curry-style kebab.

Suggested search terms: "Nepali sekuwa skewers", "grilled meat skewers Nepal", "Nepali barbecue kebab"
