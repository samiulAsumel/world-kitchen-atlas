Dhido (ढिँडो (Dhido))
Nepal - Mains

A thick, vigorously stirred porridge made from buckwheat flour and water, the traditional staple starch of Nepal's hill regions, served in place of rice alongside dal, curry, or gundruk.

Key ingredients: buckwheat flour, water, salt, ghee

What it should actually look like: A smooth, thick, glossy mound of pale grayish-brown porridge shaped into a dome on a plate, served beside small bowls of dal or gundruk -- its stiff, sculpted mound shape (not pourable like regular porridge) is the key visual cue, similar in form to polenta or ugali but with a distinct grayish-brown buckwheat color.

Suggested search terms: "dhido buckwheat porridge", "Nepali dhido plate", "Nepal traditional millet dish"
