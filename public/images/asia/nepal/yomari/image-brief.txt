Yomari (याःमरि (Yomari))
Nepal - Desserts

A steamed rice-flour dumpling pinched into a distinctive tapered, wheat-stalk shape and filled with sweet molasses (chaku) and toasted sesame, made by Nepal's Newar community for the Yomari Punhi harvest festival.

Key ingredients: rice flour, hot water, just off the boil, salt, chaku (Nepali cane molasses/jaggery), grated or chopped, sesame seeds, toasted and coarsely ground

What it should actually look like: Small, pale white, glossy steamed dumplings with a distinctive tapered, pinched point at the top (resembling a wheat stalk or fig), arranged on a plate -- the pointed taper is the key visual feature distinguishing it from any round dumpling or momo.

Suggested search terms: "yomari Nepali dumpling", "Newari rice flour dumpling", "Yomari Punhi sweet"
