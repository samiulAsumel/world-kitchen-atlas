Aloo Achar (आलु अचार)
Nepal - Sides

Boiled potato tossed in a mustard-oil dressing with toasted sesame, timur, and dried chili -- one of Nepal's most common everyday side dishes.

Key ingredients: potato, boiled and cubed, sesame seeds, mustard oil, timur (Sichuan pepper), ground, red chili powder, garlic, minced, green chili, chopped, cilantro, chopped, lemon juice, salt

What it should actually look like: A bowl of golden-brown potato cubes speckled with toasted sesame seeds and red chili flakes, glistening with mustard oil -- drier and more matte than the yogurt-based chukauni, with visible sesame texture.
