Kwati (क्वाती (Kwati))
Nepal - Soups

A hearty Newari soup made from nine or more varieties of sprouted, dried beans and legumes, simmered with ginger, garlic, and a tempering of cumin and fenugreek -- a nutrient-dense monsoon classic tied to the Gunla Punhi festival.

Key ingredients: mixed dried beans and legumes, mustard oil, cumin seeds, fenugreek seeds, ginger, garlic, tomato

What it should actually look like: A rustic, medium-brown bean soup in a bowl, with whole and split beans clearly visible in a slightly thickened broth, flecked with tempered spices and fresh coriander -- not a smooth puree, and not a thin, clear broth.

Suggested search terms: "kwati nepali bean soup", "nine bean soup nepal", "newari kwati soup"
