Thukpa (थुक्पा (Thukpa))
Nepal - Soups

A hearty, Tibetan-influenced wheat noodle soup with chicken and vegetables in a warming, gently spiced broth -- a go-to comfort food across Nepal, especially in colder regions.

Key ingredients: chicken, bone-in pieces, water, onion, sliced, garlic, minced, ginger, minced, salt, wheat noodles (fresh or dried), carrot, julienned, cabbage, shredded, spring onion, chopped, tomato, chopped, soy sauce, vegetable oil, black pepper, ground, chili flakes

What it should actually look like: A deep bowl of noodle soup with visible wheat noodles submerged in a pale golden broth, topped with shredded chicken, carrot, cabbage, and scattered spring onion -- a hearty, brothy bowl distinct from a dry noodle stir-fry.
