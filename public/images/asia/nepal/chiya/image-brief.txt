Chiya (चिया)
Nepal - Drinks

Nepal's everyday milk tea -- black tea simmered directly with milk, water, sugar, and warming spices like ginger and cardamom until rich and fragrant.

Key ingredients: water, whole milk, black tea leaves (CTC or loose leaf), sugar, ginger, crushed, cardamom pod, lightly crushed

What it should actually look like: A small glass of rich caramel-brown milk tea with a thin layer of light froth on top -- distinctly darker and more opaque than black tea, served in a small glass rather than a large mug.

Suggested search terms: "Chiya Nepali food", "Chiya recipe Nepal", "traditional Nepali drinks"
