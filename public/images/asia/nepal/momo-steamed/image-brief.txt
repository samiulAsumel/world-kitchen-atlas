Momo (Steamed) (मःमः (स्टीम))
Nepal - Snacks

Nepal's iconic steamed dumpling -- a thin dough wrapper hand-pleated around seasoned minced meat, steamed until juicy, and served with a tangy tomato-sesame achar.

Key ingredients: all-purpose flour, water, salt, minced chicken, onion, very finely chopped, cabbage, very finely chopped, garlic, minced, ginger, minced, cilantro, chopped, vegetable oil, cumin powder, salt, tomato, roasted or boiled, sesame seeds, toasted, garlic, dried red chili, mustard oil, salt

What it should actually look like: A plate of plump, pale, translucent-skinned dumplings with visible hand-pleated tops, arranged in a circle or stack, served beside a small bowl of deep red-orange tomato sauce -- distinctly softer and paler than fried momo, with no golden crust.
