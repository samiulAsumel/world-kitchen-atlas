Bara (बारा (Bara))
Nepal - Breads

A savory, pan-fried lentil patty made from a smooth, whipped batter of soaked and skinned black gram (urad dal), a Newari specialty and a staple of the festive Samay Baji platter.

Key ingredients: split black gram (urad dal), skinned, water, ginger, minced, green chili, chopped, salt, mustard oil, egg

What it should actually look like: A stack or plate of flat, round, golden-brown fried lentil patties with a slightly bumpy, savory-cake surface -- pale beige interior visible at a cut edge, distinct from a smooth potato patty or a wheat-flour fritter.

Suggested search terms: "bara Newari lentil pancake", "wo Nepali lentil cake", "fried lentil patty Nepal"
