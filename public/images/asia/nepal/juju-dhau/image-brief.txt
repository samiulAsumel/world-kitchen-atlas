Juju Dhau (जुजु धौ (Juju Dhau))
Nepal - Desserts

A rich, thick, subtly sweet fermented curd traditionally made in handmade clay pots in Bhaktapur -- prized for its dense, custard-like texture and gently caramelized flavor from full-fat milk reduced before setting.

Key ingredients: whole milk, sugar, yogurt starter culture

What it should actually look like: A thick, pale caramel-colored set curd, usually presented in a wide, shallow clay pot, with a smooth, custard-like surface -- much denser and creamier-looking than thin, pourable plain yogurt.

Suggested search terms: "juju dhau bhaktapur", "king curd nepal", "bhaktapur yogurt clay pot"
