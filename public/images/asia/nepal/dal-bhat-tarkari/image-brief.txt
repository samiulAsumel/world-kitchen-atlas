Dal Bhat Tarkari (दाल भात तरकारी (Dal Bhat Tarkari))
Nepal - Mains

Nepal's everyday staple meal: steamed rice, a spiced lentil soup finished with a sizzling ghee tempering, and a seasonal vegetable curry served alongside.

Key ingredients: rice, rinsed, water, salt, yellow lentils (masoor or moong dal), rinsed, water, turmeric powder, salt, ghee, cumin seeds, garlic, thinly sliced, dried red chili, potato, peeled and cubed, cauliflower, cut into small florets, mustard oil, cumin seeds, ginger, minced, garlic, minced, tomato, chopped, turmeric powder, red chili powder, salt

What it should actually look like: A large plate or thali with a mound of white steamed rice in the center, a bowl or ladle of thin, golden-yellow lentil soup beside it, and a separate spiced potato-cauliflower curry -- three visually distinct components on one plate, not mixed together.
