Momo (Fried) (मम (Momo))
Nepal - Snacks

Nepal's best-known dumpling, steamed first for a juicy filling and then fried until the wrapper turns golden and crisp, served with a smoky tomato-sesame achar.

Key ingredients: all-purpose flour, water, room temperature, salt, vegetable oil, minced chicken (or buffalo/pork), onion, finely chopped, garlic, minced, ginger, minced, spring onion, finely chopped, soy sauce, timur (Nepali Sichuan pepper) or black pepper, ground, salt, vegetable oil, tomato, dried red chili, garlic, sesame seeds, mustard oil, salt, vegetable oil

What it should actually look like: A plate of golden-brown, crisp-shelled dumplings with visible pleated tops, arranged in a circle with a small bowl of reddish-brown tomato-sesame achar on the side -- distinct from plain white steamed momo or Chinese-style potstickers by its all-over deep-fried crispness.

Suggested search terms: "fried momo dumplings", "Nepali momo with achar", "crispy Tibetan dumplings"
