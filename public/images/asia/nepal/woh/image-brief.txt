Woh (वः)
Nepal - Snacks

A savory, deep-fried patty made from ground black lentil batter, crisp outside and soft within, eaten as a snack or breakfast item across Newari households.

Key ingredients: split black lentils (urad dal), skinned, soaked 4+ hours, water, for grinding, ginger, minced, green chili, finely chopped, salt, asafoetida (hing), vegetable oil, for frying

What it should actually look like: A stack of round, flat, deep-fried golden-brown lentil patties with a slightly bumpy, crisp-edged surface -- denser and more uniformly disc-shaped than a fritter, without visible stuffing.

Suggested search terms: "Woh Nepali food", "Woh recipe Nepal", "traditional Nepali snacks"
