Chana Chatpate (चना चटपटे)
Nepal - Snacks

Boiled chickpeas tossed with onion, tomato, chili, and a sharp lemon or tamarind dressing in Nepal's tangy chatpate style -- a popular, protein-rich everyday snack.

Key ingredients: black chickpeas (kala chana), boiled until tender, onion, finely chopped, tomato, finely chopped, green chili, finely chopped, cilantro, chopped, chatpate spice mix (cumin, chili powder, black salt), lemon juice, mustard oil, salt

What it should actually look like: A bowl of dark brown-black chickpeas tossed with finely chopped red tomato, white onion, and green cilantro, glistening with a thin, tangy dressing -- a chunky, textured salad rather than a smooth dish.
