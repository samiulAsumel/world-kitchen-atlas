Aloo Tama (आलु तामा (Aloo Tama))
Nepal - Sides

A tangy, hearty curry of potato and fermented bamboo shoots (tama), often simmered with black-eyed peas (bodi) -- a beloved, comforting Nepali household dish.

Key ingredients: fermented bamboo shoot (tama), sliced, potato, peeled and cubed, black-eyed peas (bodi), cooked or canned, tomato, chopped, mustard oil, cumin seeds, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric powder, red chili powder, salt, water, cilantro, chopped

What it should actually look like: A bowl of potato cubes and pale yellowish-brown fermented bamboo shoot strands in a thin, reddish tomato-based gravy with visible black-eyed peas -- the shredded bamboo shoot strands are the distinguishing visual detail versus a plain potato curry.

Suggested search terms: "aloo tama curry", "Nepali bamboo shoot potato curry", "tama bodi curry"
