Choila (छ्वेला (Choila))
Nepal - Snacks

A smoky, spice-marinated Newari grilled meat dish -- traditionally buffalo, though chicken and goat versions are common -- finished with a mustard-oil tempering and served with beaten rice.

Key ingredients: chicken (or buffalo/goat), mustard oil, garlic, ginger, dried red chili, timur, tomato

What it should actually look like: Charred, dark reddish-brown chopped meat pieces glistening with mustard oil and specked with red chili and green herbs, served on a plate, often alongside flattened beaten rice -- distinctly dry and tossed, not saucy or stewed like a curry.

Suggested search terms: "choila nepali grilled meat", "newari choila", "nepali spicy grilled chicken choila"
