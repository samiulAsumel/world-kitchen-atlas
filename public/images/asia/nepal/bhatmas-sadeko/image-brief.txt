Bhatmas Sadeko (भटमास सडेको)
Nepal - Snacks

Roasted soybeans tossed warm with onion, tomato, chili, and mustard oil in the classic Nepali sadeko style -- a simple, protein-rich bar and household snack.

Key ingredients: dried soybeans, soaked a few hours and drained, mustard oil, onion, finely chopped, tomato, finely chopped, green chili, finely chopped, garlic, minced, timur (Sichuan pepper), ground, lemon juice, cilantro, chopped, salt

What it should actually look like: A small bowl of roasted, slightly wrinkled golden-brown soybeans tossed with chopped raw onion, tomato, and cilantro -- the beans should look dry-roasted and crisp, not boiled or soft.
