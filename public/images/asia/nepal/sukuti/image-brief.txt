Sukuti (सुकुटी (Sukuti))
Nepal - Snacks

Sun-dried, smoked strips of meat -- traditionally buffalo or goat -- rehydrated and pan-fried, then tossed with garlic, chili, and mustard oil into a punchy, chewy bar snack.

Key ingredients: dried meat (buffalo or goat), mustard oil, garlic, ginger, dried red chili, onion, tomato

What it should actually look like: Dark brown, slightly crispy-edged strips or pieces of fried dried meat tossed with visible tempering (sliced onion, tomato, chili), served as a snack plate -- noticeably chewier and drier-looking than a fresh meat curry or choila.

Suggested search terms: "sukuti nepali dried meat", "sukuti sadeko", "nepali dried meat fry"
