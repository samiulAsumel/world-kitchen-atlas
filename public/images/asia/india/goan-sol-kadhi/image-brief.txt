Dish: Goan Sol Kadhi (Kokum and Coconut Milk Drink)
Slug: goan-sol-kadhi

What to shoot:
- A small bowl or short glass of sol kadhi — pale salmon-pink, thin, milky
  coconut liquid (color comes from kokum, NOT deep red/magenta)
- Garnish: a few curry leaves or fresh coriander leaves floating on top,
  sometimes a couple of dried kokum petals visible
- Clean, simple background (light wood, marble, or plain ceramic)
- Top-down or 3/4 angle, natural light preferred
