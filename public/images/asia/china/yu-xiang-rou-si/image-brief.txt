Yu Xiang Rou Si (鱼香肉丝)
China - Mains

Shredded pork stir-fried with wood ear mushroom and vegetables in Sichuan's classic "fish-fragrant" sauce — a pickled-chili-based sweet-sour-savory sauce that traditionally seasoned fish dishes, giving the sauce its name even though the dish itself contains no fish at all.

Key ingredients: pork loin (julienned), wood ear mushroom, bamboo shoot or carrot, pickled chili (paocai/pixian chili), garlic, ginger, scallion, black vinegar, sugar, soy sauce

What it should actually look like: fine matchstick-thin shreds of pale pork mixed with dark, wavy wood ear mushroom strips and thin carrot/bamboo shoot shreds, coated in a glossy reddish-brown sweet-sour-savory sauce — everything should be uniformly julienned into thin strips, not chunks.

Suggested search terms: "yu xiang rou si", "fish fragrant shredded pork Sichuan", "yuxiang pork wood ear mushroom"
