Buddha Jumps Over the Wall (佛跳墙)
China - Soups

An opulent Fuzhou banquet soup layering many luxury ingredients — dried abalone, sea cucumber, fish maw, quail eggs, mushrooms, and cured ham — slow-simmered together in a sealed clay jar (a Shaoxing wine jar, traditionally) until the broth turns deeply savory and gelatinous.

Key ingredients: dried abalone, sea cucumber, fish maw, quail eggs, shiitake mushroom, Jinhua ham, bamboo shoot, Shaoxing wine

What it should actually look like: served in a small individual clay or ceramic jar/bowl, the many ingredients visible layered or nestled together in a rich amber broth with a glossy, slightly thickened sheen — an elegant, restaurant-banquet presentation, not a casual home soup bowl.

Suggested search terms: "fo tiao qiang", "Buddha jumps over the wall soup", "Fuzhou luxury clay jar soup"
