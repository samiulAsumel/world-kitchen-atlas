Zhajiangmian (炸酱面)
China - Mains

Thick hand-pulled or machine-cut wheat noodles topped with a rich, savory sauce of ground pork slow-fried in yellow soybean paste, served with julienned raw and blanched vegetable garnishes to mix in.

Key ingredients: thick wheat noodles, ground pork, yellow soybean paste (huangjiang), sweet bean sauce (tianmianjiang), cucumber, bean sprouts, radish, scallion

What it should actually look like: A bowl of thick, pale wheat noodles topped with a glossy, deep reddish-brown minced-pork sauce, with small separate piles of julienned raw cucumber, radish, and scallion arranged around the edge rather than mixed in. The sauce should look thick and matte-glossy, not thin or soupy. This is distinct from Korean jjajangmyeon, which uses a much darker, sweeter, near-black roasted bean paste (chunjang) and often includes diced potato/onion/zucchini cooked into the sauce — avoid photos labeled as jjajangmyeon or with that darker, sweeter-looking sauce.

Suggested search terms: "zhajiangmian noodles", "Beijing fried sauce noodles", "Chinese noodles minced pork bean sauce cucumber"
