Braised Pork Trotters (卤猪蹄)
China - Mains

Pork trotters braised low and slow in a soy-based master stock (卤水, lǔshuǐ) spiced with star anise, cinnamon, and other aromatics until deeply tender, glossy, and infused all the way through.

Key ingredients: pork trotters, dark soy sauce, light soy sauce, rock sugar, star anise, cinnamon stick, bay leaf, ginger, scallion, Shaoxing wine

What it should actually look like: whole or halved pork trotters (feet), unmistakably foot-shaped with visible skin, tendon, and small bones — not a squared-off belly cut — glazed deep amber-brown and glistening from the braise, usually served whole or chopped into pieces on a plate.

Suggested search terms: "braised pig trotter lu zhu ti", "Chinese braised pork feet", "soy braised pork trotter"
