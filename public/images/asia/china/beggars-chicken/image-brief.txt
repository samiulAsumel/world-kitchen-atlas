Beggar's Chicken (叫化鸡)
China - Mains

A whole chicken stuffed with aromatics, wrapped in lotus leaves, encased in clay (or foil as a home substitute), and slow-baked until the meat is fall-apart tender and infused with the lotus leaf's fragrance; traditionally cracked open tableside.

Key ingredients: whole chicken, lotus leaves, clay (or foil substitute), Shaoxing wine, scallion, ginger, mushroom, five-spice powder, soy sauce

What it should actually look like: Either the dramatic moment of cracking open a baked clay or foil shell to reveal the lotus-leaf-wrapped chicken inside with steam rising, or the unwrapped chicken itself — golden-brown, glistening, whole or torn into pieces, with visible lotus-leaf fragments nearby. Should not look like a plain Western-style roast chicken with no wrapping context, since the packet/reveal presentation is the dish's defining visual trait.

Suggested search terms: "beggar's chicken clay baked", "jiaohua ji lotus leaf chicken", "Hangzhou clay pot chicken"
