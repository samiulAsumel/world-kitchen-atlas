Yunnan Crossing the Bridge Noodles (过桥米线)
China - Soups

A steaming bowl of rich broth sealed under a layer of hot oil, brought to the table to instantly poach paper-thin slices of raw meat and a raw egg before rice noodles and vegetables are stirred in.

Key ingredients: rice vermicelli (mixian), chicken broth, thin-sliced chicken and pork, raw egg, bean sprouts, chives, pickled mustard greens

What it should actually look like: A clear, golden broth in a deep bowl with thin rice noodles, poached thin meat slices, and a softly cooked egg, distinct from a murky or dark broth - the oil sheen on the surface is a signature visual cue.

Suggested search terms: "guoqiao mixian", "Yunnan crossing the bridge noodles", "rice noodle soup clear broth raw egg"
