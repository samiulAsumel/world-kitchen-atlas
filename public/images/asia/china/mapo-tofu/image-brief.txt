Mapo Tofu (麻婆豆腐)
China - Mains

Soft tofu cubes simmered in a fiery, numbing sauce of fermented broad bean paste, chili, and minced meat, finished with Sichuan peppercorn.

Key ingredients: soft tofu, ground pork, doubanjiang (fermented broad bean paste), fermented black beans, chili powder, garlic, ginger, scallion, Sichuan peppercorn

What it should actually look like: Cubed tofu suspended in a glossy, deep red-brown sauce with visible flecks of ground meat and a pool of bright red chili oil on the surface; scallion greens and a dusting of ground Sichuan peppercorn on top. The sauce should read as fiery mala red-brown, not orange or tomato-toned, and there should be no whole fish or fish-curry appearance — that's a different dish.

Suggested search terms: "mapo tofu", "sichuan mapo dofu", "mapo tofu ground pork chili oil"
