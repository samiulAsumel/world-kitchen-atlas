Shaanxi Roujiamo (肉夹馍)
China - Breads

Long-braised, deeply spiced pork chopped fine and stuffed into a crusty, griddle-baked flatbread with a soft, layered interior - often called China's original hamburger.

Key ingredients: braised pork belly, mo flatbread (wheat), star anise, cinnamon, soy sauce, scallion

What it should actually look like: A round, crusty golden-brown flatbread split open and stuffed with finely chopped, glossy braised pork visible at the cut edge - a compact sandwich shape, not a bun/bao.

Suggested search terms: "roujiamo", "Xian Chinese hamburger", "braised pork stuffed flatbread"
