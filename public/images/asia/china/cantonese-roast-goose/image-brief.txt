Cantonese Roast Goose (烧鹅)
China - Mains

A whole goose marinated with five-spice and vinegar, air-dried, then roasted until the skin turns deep burnished red-brown and crackling-crisp, served chopped into pieces with a tart plum or vinegar-based dipping sauce.

Key ingredients: whole goose, five-spice powder, maltose syrup, vinegar, Shaoxing wine, garlic, ginger, scallion, plum sauce

What it should actually look like: Chopped goose pieces (bone-in, skin-on) fanned on a plate, skin a deep mahogany-red and glossy with visible crackle, meat darker and richer-looking than duck, usually shown hanging in a Cantonese roast-meats window display or plated with a small dish of plum/vinegar dipping sauce.

Suggested search terms: "Cantonese roast goose", "siu ngo Hong Kong", "roast goose Hong Kong deli"
