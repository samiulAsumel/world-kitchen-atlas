Xi'an Biang Biang Noodles (𰻞𰻞面)
China - Mains

Thick, belt-wide hand-pulled wheat noodles topped with garlic, scallion, and dried chili flakes, then finished with a ladle of screaming-hot oil that sizzles the toppings into a fragrant dressing right in the bowl.

Key ingredients: wide hand-pulled wheat noodles, garlic, scallion, dried chili flakes, hot oil, black vinegar, bean sprouts, bok choy

What it should actually look like: Very wide, flat, irregular ribbon-like noodles (several centimeters across, belt-like) tossed in a glossy red-tinged oil dressing with visible chili flakes and scallion, served in a bowl - distinct from thin wheat or rice noodles.

Suggested search terms: "biang biang noodles", "wide flat Chinese noodles chili oil", "hand pulled belt noodles"
