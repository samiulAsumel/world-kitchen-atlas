Nanjing Salted Duck (南京盐水鸭)
China - Mains

Duck dry-salted then poached gently in aromatic brine, served cold and sliced, prized for its tender, faintly sweet-salty meat and clean fragrance.

Key ingredients: whole duck, coarse salt, Sichuan peppercorns, Shaoxing wine, ginger, scallion, star anise

What it should actually look like: Pale ivory-white poached duck meat (NOT browned or roasted), chopped through the bone into bite-sized pieces, skin-on, arranged neatly on a plate, often served cold or at room temperature — visually distinct from Peking Duck or any roasted/browned duck preparation.

Suggested search terms: "白斩鸭 poached duck", "Chinese cold cut duck platter", "yanshui ya sliced duck"
