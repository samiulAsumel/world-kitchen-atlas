Tea Eggs (茶叶蛋)
China - Snacks

Hard-boiled eggs with cracked (but not peeled) shells, gently simmered and then steeped in a spiced brine of black tea, soy sauce, and five-spice, producing a beautiful marbled, crackle-glazed pattern across the peeled white and a deep savory flavor throughout.

Key ingredients: eggs, black tea leaves, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, star anise, cinnamon stick, five-spice powder, sugar

What it should actually look like: peeled eggs showing a distinctive dark marbled/crackled web pattern across the egg white (from the cracked-shell steeping), tea-brown in tone, usually shown a few together, halved or whole, sometimes still sitting in their spiced brown brine.

Suggested search terms: "Chinese tea eggs marbled", "cha ye dan", "five spice tea eggs peeled"
