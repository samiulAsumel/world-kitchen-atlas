White Cut Chicken (白切鸡)
China - Mains

A Cantonese classic showcasing the chicken's own flavor with minimal seasoning: a whole chicken gently poached (never boiled hard), then plunged into ice water for a tight, jelly-like skin, served cold or room-temperature, chopped on the bone, with a ginger-scallion dipping sauce.

Key ingredients: whole chicken, ginger, scallion, salt, light soy sauce, sesame oil (in the dipping sauce)

What it should actually look like: pale, smooth, almost translucent-looking chicken skin (not browned or roasted) over moist white meat, chopped into bone-in pieces and arranged on a plate, usually with a small bowl of bright green-and-white ginger-scallion sauce alongside — the pale, unroasted skin color is the dish's key visual signature, distinguishing it from roast or soy-sauce chicken.

Suggested search terms: "bai qie ji Cantonese", "white cut chicken ginger scallion sauce", "Cantonese poached chicken chopped"
