Guilin Rice Noodles (桂林米粉)
China - Mains

Guilin's classic breakfast noodle: chewy round rice noodles dry-tossed with a savory braised pork/beef sauce, crushed peanuts, and pickled vegetables, usually with a small side bowl of clear broth.

Key ingredients: round rice noodles (米粉), braised pork or beef and its sauce, roasted peanuts, pickled green beans/radish, scallion, chili oil

What it should actually look like: a shallow bowl of glossy white-to-pale rice noodles dry-tossed (not swimming in broth) with dark savory braised meat sauce, scattered peanuts, and pickled vegetable bits on top — visually drier than a noodle soup, with the broth (if shown) served separately in a small side bowl.

Suggested search terms: "Guilin mifen", "Guilin rice noodles dry tossed", "Chinese rice noodle peanuts pickled vegetables"
