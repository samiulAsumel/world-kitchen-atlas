Xinjiang Big Plate Chicken (新疆大盘鸡)
China - Mains

A hearty Xinjiang classic of chicken pieces and potato chunks braised in a bold, mildly spicy tomato-and-chili sauce, tossed together with wide, hand-pulled belt noodles at the table.

Key ingredients: chicken thighs/drumsticks, potato, bell pepper, dried chili, Sichuan peppercorn, star anise, garlic, ginger, beer or stock, wide belt noodles (皮带面)

What it should actually look like: served heaped on one large communal plate (hence the name) — glossy reddish-brown chicken and potato chunks studded with dried red chilies and bell pepper, with pale wide flat noodles either tossed through or piled underneath to soak up the sauce. Should look rustic and generous, not a small individual portion.

Suggested search terms: "Xinjiang da pan ji", "big plate chicken noodles", "Chinese chicken potato stew belt noodles"
