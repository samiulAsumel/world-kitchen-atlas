Ants Climbing a Tree (蚂蚁上树)
China - Mains

A whimsically named Sichuan home dish: mung bean vermicelli stir-fried with seasoned minced pork in a spicy, garlicky soy-based sauce — the little bits of pork clinging to the translucent noodle strands are the "ants" on the "tree branches."

Key ingredients: mung bean vermicelli (glass noodles), minced pork, doubanjiang (chili bean paste), garlic, ginger, scallion, soy sauce

What it should actually look like: a tangle of translucent, glassy vermicelli strands with small dark specks of minced pork visibly clinging to them, in a light reddish-brown sauce — the noodles should look clear/glassy, not opaque like wheat noodles, and the pork should be finely minced, not sliced or shredded.

Suggested search terms: "ants climbing a tree Sichuan", "mung bean vermicelli minced pork stir fry", "mala fen si rou mo"
